# Edits, round 2: Your First D&D Game

Apply these edits in place to `drafts/first-dnd-game/guide.md` (your working directory's
`./guide.md`). Line numbers are from the file as it is now (version 0.1.0, the round-1
draft). Replace each listed line with the new text, keeping the table row structure: label
cells stay as they are unless the edit gives a new label. Change nothing else. Don't touch

No mannered prose: the writing must not call attention to itself. Make no assumption that the
reader is an expert.

Read `packages/editorial/skills/accidental-guide-write/references/author-feedback.md` and
`packages/editorial/skills/accidental-guide-write/references/voice.md` first (paths from the

## Why

The cold reviews (notes/review-readers.md, review-fidelity.md, review-leakage.md) found two
wrong facts (dice aren't "marked" d4 to d20, and a turn isn't "your place in the order"),
terms a first-time player can't know (DC, modifier, Initiative, round, Armor Class, Hit
Points), one overreach ("Dodge is a safe action") and a title that prints its markup. The
printed guide is 1,121 words and must come down under 1,100, so this round also drops repeats
of "you don't need every rule". Every replacement below is word for word: use it as given.
