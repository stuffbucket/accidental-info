## Why

Three cold reviews (notes/review-fidelity.md, review-readers.md,
review-format-leakage.md) read version 0.2.0. The fixes:

- The Story entry reads as if jokes are banned. Both etiquette sources allow a touch of
  clean humour.
- Paragraph 2 on page 2 repeats paragraph 1. The guide never says to tie the toast to the
  occasion (sentiment at a wedding, nostalgia at a retirement), which the sources give.
- "Everyone raises a glass together" is wrong: the person being toasted doesn't.
- The sources say "a few heartfelt sentences" for a casual toast, not one line. A dinner
  guest also needs "To our hosts" more than "To us".
- The source lets the host toast first (a prerogative), and the rule holds at weddings too.
- The steps never say when in the meal to toast, or how to get attention.
