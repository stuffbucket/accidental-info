## Why

Three cold reviews (notes/review-fidelity.md, review-readers.md,
review-format-leakage.md) read version 0.3.0. The numbers and dates hold up. The fixes:

- The 3:30 p.m. row gives a fixed time for a step the thermometer decides, and never says
  how long to rest the turkey (FSIS: 20 minutes).
- "Within two hours" has no starting point. FSIS counts two hours from cooking, so the row
  becomes a clock time.
- "Two weeks before" then says not to buy a fresh one that early. FSIS: buy a fresh one on
  the Tuesday or Wednesday.
- "Needs no thawing" says more than FSIS does about a fresh turkey.
- "The thermometer decides" personifies. "Two dishes, one oven" and "Plan around the turkey"
  read as slogans.
- The roasting timetable is named but no hours are shown.
- Helpers: the pie example clashes with the host baking pies on Wednesday, and "Confused
  with: Cooks" was a filler contrast. A potluck is the real confusion.
- Small wording against the source: "can spread", "about 30 minutes".

The series labels (Identify by, Field marks, So what, Confused with) and the subtitle
pattern stay as they are. Use each replacement word for word.
