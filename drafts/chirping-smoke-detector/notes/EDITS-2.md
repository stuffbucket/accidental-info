## Why is my smoke alarm chirping? (lines 13 to 23)

2. Line 13: A smoke alarm that gives one short chirp about every 30 to 60 seconds is asking for attention, and there’s no fire. The usual cause is a low battery. If a new battery doesn’t stop the chirp, the smoke alarm has reached the end of its life and needs replacing.
