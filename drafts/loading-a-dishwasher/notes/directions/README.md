# Other takes on Loading a Dishwasher

Eight alternative drafts of `guide.md`, for comparing side by side. Each is a complete file in the same format, with the same five entries and tiles (Bo, Tc, Cb, Ar, Dg), and could replace `guide.md` as it is. They use only facts from `../sources.md`. None of them has the terms block on the first page, since the entries cover the same ground. `guide.md` itself is unchanged.

| File | The take |
|---|---|
| `watcher.md` | Told by the person at the kitchen table while you load, who loads the forks the other way and has done for years. |
| `anti-virtuoso.md` | For anyone who thinks a dishwasher needs a perfect load. Each part says what to get right and what to leave alone, and nobody claps either way. |
| `how-it-felt.md` | Each part of the dishwasher next to what people forget about it by Friday and what they still remember a year later. |
| `dishwasher.md` | The dishwasher explains how to load it, what it notices and what it doesn’t. |
| `zoom-out.md` | Each part seen up close, from the kitchen table and over a year, ending with what a dishwasher saves in a year. |
| `two-loaders.md` | One load told by two people who put the cutlery in differently, with a maker’s manual backing each of them on every point. |
| `favourite-argument.md` | Handles up or handles down: the makers don’t agree, so the forks get the whole guide and the rest of the dishwasher is the warm-up. |
| `scorecard.md` | Every rack scored by where the makers disagree, with the card on the fridge kept over eight years. |
