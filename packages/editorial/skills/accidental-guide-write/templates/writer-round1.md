## Length

Five minutes front to back: about 1,000 words in guide.md, tables and sources included,
which prints as about 1,100. Stay under the word budget for each page in the plan. Cut
detail, not clarity.

## How to write it

- Regular English, normal word order, "you" where that's the natural way to say it.
- Build one idea at a time. Each paragraph answers the reader's next question, and each
  sentence follows from the last. Keep one subject running through a passage.
- No paragraph by list: if it's a list, it goes in a table. Otherwise write connected
  sentences.
- Short, but not staccato.
- Every term is defined before or where it's first used.
- Each key concept has one name. Use it every time.
- No quotes and no names of people. No non-public company material of any kind.
- Cross-references in the colon form: "An Accidental Guide to: Agents, Harnesses, Skills,
  and Tools".
- The form of the guide (entries, field marks) is structure only. Don't explain it or use its
  vocabulary in the text.

## Keep the shape

Keep the front matter keys, every heading and its `{#id ...}`, the number of paragraphs in
each section, table header rows, first-column labels and the field mark labels. Don't add or
remove sections. Don't touch the Sources section unless the plan says to.
